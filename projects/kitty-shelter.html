01

About

Kitty Shelter is a cozy simulation and management game about running a cat shelter. It connects what players do in the game with real cats and the people who rescue them.

We’re seeking a shelter partner to advise development and co-create authentic cat characters & stories!

02

How it works

In real life

Scan a cat

Take photos with a smartphone or smart glasses. “Hey Glasses, scan this cat and generate a profile.”

Database

Match a profile

Cat information and profile matching feed a database that provides information for non-profit rescue & TNR organizations.

In game

Earn rewards

Contributions come back as in-game rewards in the shelter simulation, with potential future donations.
